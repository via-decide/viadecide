# Building a Decision Engine for the Internet

## 

## 

## 

Most people don’t struggle with information.

They struggle with  **decisions.** 

Every day we ask questions like:

- 

Which phone should I buy?

- 

How do I research a topic properly?

- 

What AI prompt will give the best result?

- 

Which option is actually better?

The internet gives us  **infinite information** , but very little help in  **structuring decisions** .

So I started building something called  **ViaDecide — a Decision Engine.** 

## The Idea

Instead of jumping across multiple websites, tools, and tabs, what if decisions could be structured inside a single system?

That system could help you:

- 

break down complex choices

- 

organize research

- 

compare real options

- 

experiment with ideas

- 

even learn through simulations and games

This is the thinking behind ViaDecide.

## What I’m Building

Right now the platform includes a collection of small “decision tools”:

⚗️  **Alchemist**  – turn messy thoughts into structured decisions

🧠  **Memory Engine**  – store and reuse research

🛒  **ONDC Demo / Engine Deals**  – explore real product choices

📚  **StudyOS**  – structured research workflow for students

🎮  **HexWars & Multiplayer Quiz Experiments**  – learning through interactive simulations

⚡  **Prompt Alchemy**  – craft better prompts for AI tools

Each tool explores a different way to  **improve decision quality** .

## Why This Matters

We spend years learning subjects in school.

But almost no time learning  **how to make better decisions** .

Better decisions can improve:

- 

learning

- 

buying choices

- 

career paths

- 

research

- 

entrepreneurship

The goal of this project is simple:

 **Build tools that help people think better.**
