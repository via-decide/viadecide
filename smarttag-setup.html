SmartTag Setup

Configure your hardware in 60 seconds

Step 1: Get the App

Download 'NFC Tools' to write the data to your SmartTag.

App Store (iOS)
Google Play (Android)

Step 2: Copy Payload

Copy your unique Explorer Pass URL below. This is what unlocks the ecosystem.

https://viadecide.com/launcher

Step 3: Write to Tag

Follow these exact steps in the NFC Tools app to write your payload.

1

Open NFC Tools and tap Write.

2

Tap Add a record > URL / URI.

3

Paste the copied URL and tap OK.

4

Tap Write / X Bytes and tap your phone to the SmartTag.

All Set!

Your SmartTag is now ready. Tap it with any smartphone to instantly launch the ViaDecide ecosystem.
