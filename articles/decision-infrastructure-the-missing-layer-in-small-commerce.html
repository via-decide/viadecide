2. THESIS

They need a system.

Right now the average seller stack looks like this:

• Shopify • WhatsApp • Google Sheets • Marketplace dashboards • Random analytics tools

Tools everywhere. Decisions nowhere.

This is why most small brands stay trapped in low margins, platform dependency, and chaotic growth.

The problem isn’t technology.

The problem is decision infrastructure.

3. DEPTH

What Decision Infrastructure Actually Means

Decision infrastructure is the system that converts messy information into clear actions.

A simple model:

Input Data, signals, prices, inventory, user behavior.

Intent What decision actually matters right now?

Examples:

  • Should I change pricing?

  • Should I list on ONDC?

  • Should I promote this product?

State Current context.

Examples:

  • inventory level

  • margin

  • customer demand

  • logistics cost

Output Clear decision.

Examples:

  • increase price by ₹50

  • run offer for 48 hours

  • stop marketplace dependency

Without this loop, businesses operate on guessing.

4. INSIGHT

Why SaaS Alone Doesn’t Fix This

SaaS tools optimize tasks.

But commerce success depends on decisions.

Example:

A dashboard can show:

  • traffic

  • conversions

  • revenue

But it rarely answers the real question:

“What should I do next?”

That’s where decision infrastructure matters.
