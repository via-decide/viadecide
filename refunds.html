Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. General Policy

Due to the cryptographic and offline nature of our sovereign software licenses and personalized physical products, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. If a generated passport or environment token fails to authenticate due to our system errors within 14 days of purchase, you are eligible for a full refund.

2. 3D-Printed & Hardware Products

For custom 3D-printed items (such as NFC Keychains and custom numberplates), since each product is manufactured specifically to your design configurations, cancellations are only accepted before the physical printing process begins. If the item is defective or damaged during transit, we will dispatch a replacement at zero additional cost.

3. Phygital Bundles & Vault Activation

Physical packages containing digital bundle access codes (including the SmartTag Lite) are subject to specific return exclusions. Activation or redemption of the bundled digital vault code on Aporaksha.com explicitly waives the customer's right to claim a return or refund on the accompanying physical product. Upon vault activation, the transaction is locked and converted into a final, non-refundable digital sale.

4. Processing

Approved refunds are processed through our secure payment gateway partner (Razorpay) and typically reflect in your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

5. Contact Us

To request a refund or report a damaged product delivery, please email dharam@viadecide.com with your transaction ID, order details, and details of the issue.
